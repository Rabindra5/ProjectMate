Privacy policy

How ProjectMate handles your information.

Overview

ProjectMate is an academic project developed by Group 15 at the University of Windsor for COMP-8347. We respect your privacy and aim to provide a responsible environment for student collaboration.

Information we collect

ProjectMate may collect information that you provide or generate while using the platform, including:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Technical skills and project information.
  • Team memberships and join requests.
  • Search and browsing activity.

How we use your information

Information is used to support account management, project discovery, teammate searches, recommendations and collaboration within ProjectMate.

Cookies and sessions

ProjectMate uses cookies and sessions to maintain login sessions, remember preferences and support website functionality.

Your information

You can manage your profile and skills through your account settings. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal or confidential information in publicly visible project content.

Contact us

If you have questions about privacy, please contact a member of our ProjectMate team.
