About ProjectMate

Connecting students. Creating opportunities. Building together.

Our project

ProjectMate is a student collaboration platform developed at the University of Windsor. It provides a centralized space for students to discover projects, showcase their skills, find teammates and collaborate on shared ideas.

Our goal is to make project discovery and team formation simpler, more accessible and more efficient for students with diverse interests and technical backgrounds.
